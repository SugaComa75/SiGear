Content Moderation Policy — SiGear (Draft)

Scope:
- Applies to content intentionally shared to the SiGear "safe network" and flagged content originating from pilot users.

Principles:
- Keep allowed speech broad while removing content that is illegal, exploitative, or clearly harmful to minors.
- Prioritise human review for ambiguous cases.
- Provide clear notices and appeals for users/parents.

Categories and actions:
- Illegal content (CSAM, terrorism, etc.): immediate takedown and referral to authorities.
- Sexual content involving minors: immediate block + mandatory reporting.
- Harassment/bullying aimed at minors: escalate to human review and apply age-protective measures.
- Self-harm content: triage with supportive resources and optional parent alert depending on consent and local policy.

Moderation workflow:
1. Automated classifier assigns severity score and category.
2. High-severity (illegal) items auto-quarantined and routed to emergency queue.
3. Medium/low severity items routed to human review with context and explainability metadata.
4. Reviewer decisions logged with rationale; appeals pipeline available.

Transparency:
- Publish moderation policy summary and reporting channels for the pilot.
- Provide parents with summary logs and options for appeals.

Reviewer training:
- Provide clear guidelines, sample cases, and escalation paths to legal experts.
- Maintain reviewer rotation and wellbeing support (sensitive content handling).
