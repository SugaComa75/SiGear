Monitoring, Alerts & Reporting — SiGear

Monitoring:
- Metrics: service uptime, queue lengths, classifier performance, review SLA
- Logs: Hub local logs (encrypted), server access logs, moderation action logs

Alerts:
- Real-time alerts for critical failures, surge in high-severity flags, or SOS events
- Escalation routing to on-call engineer, legal team, and emergency contacts as appropriate

Reporting:
- Weekly pilot summary dashboards for internal and regulator review
- Aggregated, pseudonymised analytics for safety outcomes
- Exportable incident reports (for ICO/Ofcom) with audit trails

Access & security:
- Role-based dashboards, least privilege, and audit logging for report access
