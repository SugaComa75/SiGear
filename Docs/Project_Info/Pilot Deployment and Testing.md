Pilot Deployment & Testing — Plan (Draft)

Pilot goals:
- Validate functionality, privacy flows, moderation accuracy, and parent experience.

Scope:
- 50–100 volunteer households + 1–2 partner schools (adjustable)
- Duration: 4–8 weeks

Activities:
- Recruitment and consent
- Baseline surveys and ongoing feedback collection
- Technical monitoring: uptime, logs, moderation metrics
- Safety incident handling and escalation

Success criteria:
- Stable service with <5% critical failures
- Human review SLA met
- Positive qualitative feedback from parents and teachers

Pilot ops:
- Dedicated ops channel, incident playbooks, on-call engineer
- Regular check-ins and data export for ICO/regulator review
