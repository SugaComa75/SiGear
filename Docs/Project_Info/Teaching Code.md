### What should absolutely be baked into the system.



Not as a rule.



As a skill kids unlock and learn.



#### Imagine this inside SIGEAR:



*Module: “Your Online Identity”*



* Create a public persona



* Create a private persona



* Learn what belongs in each



* Simulate consequences of mixing them



Then later:



*Module: “Reputation \& Risk”*



* What happens when identities merge



* When it’s okay



* When it’s dangerous



And eventually:



*Advanced: “Digital Footprint Strategy”*



* Employers



* Data brokers



* Long-term visibility





The quiet win here



We don't just keep our kids safe.



We raise people who can navigate risk without needing supervision.



That’s the whole game.



SIGEAR, shouldn’t create kids who need it forever.



It should create kids who eventually go:



“I don’t need this anymore”



Which, from a business perspective, is hilariously self-destructive.



From a human perspective, it’s exactly right.
