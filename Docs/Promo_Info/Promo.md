## Idea
Each person has a secure identity (**NTI**) paired with an adaptive companion (**Avatar**) and an enforcement layer (**SiGear**) that:

- understands who they are
- applies what’s appropriate
- guides what happens next

---

## Roles (clear separation)
- **NTI (authority):** The *only* source of consent/policy decisions. Apps do **not** interpret consent.
- **SiGear (runtime enforcement):** Enforces NTI policy between apps and protected resources.
- **Avatar (user-facing companion):** Explains, guides, and reflects NTI state; it does **not** grant permissions.
- **Apps (requesters):** Request capabilities/services; access is granted or denied by NTI policy via SiGear enforcement.
