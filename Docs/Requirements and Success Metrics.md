Requirements & Success Metrics — SiGear MVP

MVP Requirements (functional):
- Hub: per-device profiles, DNS policy enforcement, local logs, SOS relay
- Mobile App: safe social feed viewer, VPN client, SOS button, opt-in sharing
- Cloud: moderation queue, automated classifier, human review UI, audit logs
- Parent Dashboard: profile management, alerts, analytics, block/allow lists

Non-functional:
- UK data residency, encrypted at rest/in transit, 99.9% availability for core services
- Explainability for automated flags, max 24-hour human review SLA
- Secure firmware / signed updates for Hub

Success Metrics (proposed):
- Adoption: 100 pilot households in phase-1
- Moderation SLA: 90% of flagged items reviewed within 24 hours
- Accuracy: automated classifier precision > 85% (pilot-adjusted)
- User satisfaction: parent NPS > 40
- Safety outcome: measurable reduction in reported harmful exposures during pilot (qualitative + quantitative)
