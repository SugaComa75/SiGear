Stakeholder & Legal Review — SiGear MVP

Summary:
- Objective: map regulatory, legal, and stakeholder obligations for the SiGear platform in the UK.

Key legal areas:
- UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018 compliance
- Children's Online Privacy Protection: parental consent, age-appropriate design

- Criminal law obligations for content (terrorism, child sexual abuse imagery, etc.) — mandatory reporting channels

Stakeholders:
- Regulators: ICO, Ofcom, DCMS
- Public: parents, schools, child-safety NGOs
- Platform partners: social networks, VPN/DNS providers
- Internal: legal counsel, privacy engineer, product lead

- Engage ICO early for feedback on data flows and retention
